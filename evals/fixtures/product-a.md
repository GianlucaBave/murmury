# Lumo L2 Desk Lamp

## Overview
The Lumo L2 is a dimmable LED desk lamp with a flexible arm and a weighted base. It is made for home
offices and study desks.

## Specifications
- Light output: 800 lumens
- Colour temperature: adjustable from 2700 K to 6000 K
- Power: 9 W, USB-C cable included
- Height: 42 cm with the arm fully raised
- Weight: 1.1 kg
- Colours: matte white or graphite

## Price
Price: $120 per lamp, tax not included.

## In the box
- Lumo L2 lamp
- USB-C cable, 1.5 m
- Quick start card
